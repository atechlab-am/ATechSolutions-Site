Tether

IT Asset Management for MSPs

Tether helps MSPs and their clients track, manage, and report on IT assets from one platform. It is multi-tenant by design, supports QR labels, and includes client-facing branded portals.

Key features

  • Multi-tenant architecture with isolated client workspaces
  • QR asset labels for quick scan-and-view in the field
  • CSV import/export for bulk onboarding and reporting
  • Role-based permissions with per-user overrides
  • Branded client portals with white-label subdomains
  • Warranty tracking, check-out/check-in, and request workflows

For MSPs

Manage all clients from one dashboard with visibility on asset counts, values, and expiring warranties.

For Clients

Each client gets a dedicated portal to view assets, assignments, and requests without MSP complexity.

Deployment Options

Use hosted SaaS or self-host for full control. Tether is open source under the MIT license.
