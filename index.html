How it works

Three steps to IT that stays out of your way

1. Assess

An IT health check reviews your devices, email, network, and backups, and tells you exactly where you stand.

2. Stabilize

What's included

The six things every managed plan covers

Monitoring & patching

Your devices are watched for issues and kept up to date, so small problems get caught before they become downtime.

Verified backups

Backups are set up and checked — not just running, but actually restorable if you ever need them.

Helpdesk support

A real person to call when something isn't working, with priority response for managed plan clients.

Security basics

Multi-factor authentication, endpoint protection, and account hardening — the fundamentals most offices are missing.

Documentation

Your systems, accounts, and configurations are documented, so you're never dependent on one person's memory.

Quarterly review
